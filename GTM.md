# Go-to-market — from preview to $1M run rate

The pad's value is not the website. It is (1) a scarce, defended set of launch rights and (2) the only
public, verifiable record of memecoin developer behaviour. Everything below follows from those two.

## Sequence
1. **Scanner first, pad second.** Ship the free scanner on the live engine before the first seat exists.
   It is useful on day one to every trader and every developer on Solana, needs no seats, and seeds the
   index (`deployer_cluster` only gets better with volume). Every result card carries the brand.
2. **Founding vetting round in public.** Open applications, publish the bench as it fills, run the
   72-hour public reviews in the open. The vetting *is* the content: 148 applications → 20 seats is a
   story people follow for weeks.
3. **Devnet season with real rules, fake money.** One full 30-day season on devnet with the leaderboard,
   slots, reports and a planted ban, so the first mainnet season has a visible track record.
4. **Mainnet season 1** with the program audited and the seat record visible to anyone on chain.

## Revenue (see the model on the site, `Business`)
| Stream | Starts | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Launch fees (treasury 50 %) | season 1 | 3 SOL per launch, on chain |
| Franchise $4,000 | after two clean seasons | the rulebook, program and scanner on another chain under licence; the licensee runs vetting, we run the engine |

At 1.4 launches/day the treasury share is about $72K a year at $150/SOL. Franchising one chain adds $48K ARR per
licensee with almost no marginal cost, so the path to more runs through franchises and the volume of launches. There are no subscriptions: the scanner, feeds and bot are free and exist to make the record visible.

## Distribution
- The result card and the badge are the growth loops: every scan, launch and season recap is a shareable
  image with the brand on it.
- `@SolvetScan`-style mention replies on X (scan on request) convert searchers into Pro users.
- Terminal partners (trading terminals, Telegram trading bots) embed the verification endpoint; their
  users see "Verified · Cadre" next to a ticker and come to the seat page.
- The public log and the committee record make every ban a news event. Bans are rare by design,
  which is what makes them newsworthy.

## What kills it
- **Collusion among the twenty** → §8 clustering, both seats removed, recall.
- **Committee capture** → one-season terms, recall by seats, holdings cap, public votes.
- **Score gaming** → holder outcome weighs most; survival alone cannot carry a seat.
- **A ban that does not repay holders** → say it plainly: a ban removes the developer and cannot be bought off; nobody is promised a payout.
- **Nobody applies** → the scanner is useful without seats; the bench is public and ranked.
